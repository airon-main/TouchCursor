## Pictogrammers Material Design Icons

Stock cursor vector assets come from Pictogrammers Material Design Icons and are converted to Android VectorDrawable paths without changing their geometry. The KeyCursor-specific Inward corners reticle is original project artwork and is covered by the MIT project license.

Used glyphs:

- `cursor-default`
- `cursor-pointer`
- `crosshairs`
- `target`
- `circle-outline`
- `plus`
- `crop-free`
- `image-filter-center-focus`
